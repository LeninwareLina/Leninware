# RFC-0003: Web-and-Gravity Philosophy
## The Web-and-Gravity Model

---

## 1. Introduction
The "Web-and-Gravity" framework is the foundational philosophical model guiding the design of Leninware, ContentForge, and Commonwealth. It outlines how technological infrastructure is leveraged to challenge capitalist networks of power.

---

## 2. Core Principles

### 2.1 Power is Relational, Not Monolithic
* **The Insight:** Power is an unstable, dynamic web of interacting nodes (science, military, finance, media). No single entity completely controls the system. Capitalist power survives by maintaining a "flexible center" that absorbs and neutralizes radical ideas while protecting the underlying owner-vs-worker division.
* **The Application:** ContentForge and Commonwealth treat corporate and state power as vulnerable networks rather than impenetrable castles. ContentForge exposes the "switching points" (e.g., how real estate funding warps housing policy), while Commonwealth exploits financial gaps to build local economic self-reliance.

### 2.2 Statehood and Infrastructure are Externalized Mind
* **The Insight:** Borders, laws, algorithms, and ledgers are the human mind physically carving its conceptual logic into reality to enforce predictability and combat entropy.
* **The Application:** We are engaged in reification — turning leftist, life-centric thought into digital infrastructure. ContentForge carves out an intellectual boundary to protect working-class attention from corporate neutrality, while Commonwealth constructs alternative, physical ledgers of predictability outside of capitalist banking rails.

### 2.3 Evading the "Theory Trap" via Grounded Simplicity
* **The Insight:** Academic complexity is frequently used to isolate radical ideas from the working class, rendering power paralyzing and abstract. Grounded movements demand clear, actionable answers.
* **The Application:** ContentForge rejects academic insularity. Its primary cognitive function is translation: taking dense economic or geopolitical facts and outputting clear, class-conscious truths. Commonwealth prioritizes radically intuitive interfaces; if mutual aid volunteers or tenants cannot use it instantly, it fails.

### 2.4 Overcoming Technological Determinism Through Alliances
* **The Insight:** The future is not an inevitable slide into AI-directed technocracy. Multipolar realities force every automated node to map its environment and build alliances to survive.
* **The Application:** ContentForge and Commonwealth are not isolated tools. They form an alliance with the "Life Node" (the biological survival imperative of the living collective mind). By fusing Commonwealth's material data (wage theft, evictions) with ContentForge's media engine, we create a direct loop of agitation and organization.
