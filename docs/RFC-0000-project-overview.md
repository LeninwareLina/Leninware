# RFC-0000: Leninware Overview
## Mission and System Architecture

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Leftist organizations are historically bottlenecked by propaganda, synthesis, communication, and resource coordination. Activists and organizers spend disproportionate time translating dense theoretical concepts or managing basic administrative and material logistics rather than building direct working-class power.

---

## 2. Thesis
Large Language Models (LLMs) and workflow automation are strongest precisely in the domains of synthesis, register translation, rapid media generation, and structured resource tracking. By leveraging these tools, we can alleviate systemic organizing bottlenecks.

---

## 3. Objective
Use AI to augment political capacity rather than replace organization. We construct "cognitive infrastructure" that empowers organizers, reduces administrative toil, and expands the reach of class-conscious education and agitation.

---

## 4. Projects
Leninware consists of two primary, companion projects:

* **ContentForge:** A political intelligence and media production engine. Handles:
  * Media generation (scripts, short-form, long-form videos)
  * Discourse analysis (factual and strategic analysis of opposing/mainstream messaging)
  * Register selection (tuning communication to specific audience profiles)
  * Video production (automated TTS, asset assembly, and subtitle burning)
* **Commonwealth:** A resource coordination ledger. Handles:
  * Financial organization and ledger management
  * Resource planning and logistics mapping
  * Collective provisioning and mutual aid tracking

---

## 5. Architectural Philosophy
* **AI as Force Multiplier:** AI agents and pipelines execute heavy labor (writing, rendering, mapping), but human organizers remain responsible for overall strategy, political lines, and direct physical organization.
* **Materialist Focus:** Content and decisions are grounded in material conditions (incentives, structures, resources) rather than moralizing or individual villain/hero narratives.

---

## 6. Anti-Goals
* **Artificial General Intelligence (AGI):** We are not building or chasing general consciousness.
* **Replacing Organizers:** Organizers are irreplaceable. The tool serves them, not vice versa.
* **Fully Autonomous Politics:** No automated policy decisions or machine-led lines.
* **Leaderless Algorithmic Governance:** AI does not dictate democratic collective direction.

---

## 7. Current Status
* **ContentForge:** Level 1 (Short-Form Video Production) is operational.
* **Commonwealth:** In planning/prototyping stages.
* **Next Steps:** [RFC-0001](RFC-0001-contentforge-next-horizon.md) describes the detailed expansion of ContentForge to Levels 2-4.
